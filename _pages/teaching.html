--- layout: single title: "Teaching & Mentorship" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- ## Teaching philosophy My approach to teaching is centered on active inquiry, experiential learning, and mentorship. I aim to cultivate curiosity about the natural world while helping students become independent thinkers capable of critically evaluating questions about Earth's history, life, and environments. I encourage students to engage directly with scientific observations, data analysis, and collaborative problem-solving. I also use student feedback to adapt activities and help learners turn observations into testable hypotheses. ## Classroom and laboratory teaching Across thirteen semesters of undergraduate and graduate teaching appointments at Hofstra University and the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign, I have supported laboratory, classroom, and online instruction. Courses include Honors Organismal Biology, Honors Ecology and Evolution, Ecology, The Biology of Dinosaurs, and Introductory Biology. I developed laboratory activities using paleobotanical, herbarium, and entomological collections, and field exercises investigating tallgrass prairie biodiversity and ecological processes. ## Field-based education As teaching faculty with the Juneau Icefield Research Program in 2026, I developed field lessons on primary succession in deglaciated environments and island biogeography on nunataks. These lessons integrated plant and insect surveys, hypothesis testing, field sampling, and data interpretation. ## Mentorship I have mentored twelve undergraduate researchers and one graduate student in paleobiology, statistical modeling, and machine learning. I believe students, particularly graduate students, should have the intellectual freedom to pursue questions they are passionate about. My role is to provide guidance, support, and opportunities for independent scientific growth. Three undergraduate mentees completed senior thesis projects and presented their findings through posters or oral presentations. ## Teaching recognition - University of Illinois Teachers Ranked as Excellent, 2020–2023 - Minor in College Education, University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign - Teaching certificates through the Center for Innovation in Teaching & Learning - Maximizing Mentoring Relationships training (CIMER)
